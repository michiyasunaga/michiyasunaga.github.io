Michihiro Yasunaga

Thanks for stopping by my website!

I am a 3rd-year undergraduate student at Yale University, studying computer science and statistics. I conduct research in natural language processing (NLP) and machine learning (ML), advised by Prof. Dragomir Radev (@ LILY lab) and Prof. John Lafferty.

My research focuses on developing machine learning techniques for natural language understanding tasks such as summarization (YZMPSR'17), semantic parsing, and mathematical text processing.
I am also interested in the robustness and interpretability of machine learning techniques (especially of neural networks) in NLP (YKR'18).
